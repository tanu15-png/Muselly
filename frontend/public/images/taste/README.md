# Taste-test assets

`weekend-*.svg` and `vacation-*.svg` are original project illustrations.
`outfits.png` is an original AI-generated editorial contact sheet, generated
with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-10. It contains no retailer logos,
product listings, or claims about available products. These examples illustrate
options; they do not define anyone's identity or limit their style.

The sheet is rendered as sixteen square crops in CSS, without remote image
requests. Its generation prompt was:

> Create one editorial fashion contact sheet asset for Muselly taste quiz.
> Exact 4 columns x 4 rows equally sized edge-to-edge rectangular panels, no
> margins no gutters no text no borders. Each panel full-body adult model head
> to shoes centered with space at edges, realistic fashion photography in
> natural soft light on warm neutral studio backdrop. Diverse genders and skin
> tones, inclusive, tasteful. In strict row-major order: 1 minimal quiet luxury
> cream knit black trousers; 2 streetwear baggy jeans graphic jacket sneakers;
> 3 soft feminine blush floral midi dress; 4 Y2K metallic cropped jacket
> low-rise jeans; 5 bohemian earth-tone maxi dress fringe; 6 vintage retro 1970s
> flared trousers patterned shirt; 7 dark academia brown tweed blazer plaid
> skirt loafers; 8 old money navy blazer cream trousers loafers; 9 sporty casual
> tracksuit sneakers; 10 traditional ethnic Indian embroidered kurta dupatta
> trousers; 11 oversized relaxed extra-wide jeans oversized shirt; 12 fitted
> bodycon burgundy long fitted dress; 13 flowy loose wide flowing palazzo and
> blouse; 14 structured tailored sharp grey trouser suit; 15 straight classic
> straight-leg jeans white tee; 16 experimental asymmetric layered colorful
> sculptural outfit. Consistent professional framing, each outfit unmistakable,
> no brands or logos. High resolution square contact sheet.
